Task 17 OpenFOAM 11 diagnose-fix-rerun report

The initial command-line run used OpenFOAM Foundation Version 11 and returned code 1. The original log.blockMesh.failed records a FOAM FATAL IO ERROR: block hex (0 3 2 1 4 7 6 5) is inside-out. This is the first topology fault. The repair changes that block to the standard right-handed ordering hex (0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7), while preserving the requested 20 by 20 by 1 cell counts and grading.

After only the handedness repair, the next blockMesh run returned code 1 and identified the second fault exactly: Point label 99 is out of range 0..7 on movingWall face 1. Vertex 99 does not exist. That face, (0 1 99 4), is also unnecessary because the legal bottom face is already assigned to fixedWalls. The fix removes only the invalid movingWall face; every exterior face then belongs to one appropriate patch.

The third fault is the duplicate coordinate for vertex 7. In the broken dictionary, vertex 7 repeats vertex 6 as (1 1 0.1), collapsing one top-back edge instead of closing the left side of the block. A diagnostic intermediate mesh could be written after the first two fixes, but checkMesh exposed the damage: only 380 cells remained hexahedra, 20 became wedges, 400 edges were not aligned with the two-dimensional directions, 142 faces were severely non-orthogonal, maximum skewness reached 20.4779, and three mesh checks failed. Vertex 7 was therefore corrected to (0 1 0.1).

Final validation was performed entirely from the shell in the real OpenFOAM 11 instance. blockMesh completed successfully with 882 points, 400 cells, 400 hexahedra and six fully assigned boundary faces. checkMesh reported Mesh OK with zero non-orthogonality and unit maximum aspect ratio. icoFoam then ran without fatal errors through time 0.5 s and ended normally. The fixed case retains non-empty ASCII U and p field files at time 0.5, demonstrating that the repaired mesh supports the complete transient solver run rather than merely passing a textual dictionary check.
