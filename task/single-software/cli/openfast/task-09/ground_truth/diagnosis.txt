The first initialization failure originates in broken_elasto.dat. Its NumBl field is set to 4 even though this OpenFAST turbine and ElastoDyn support three blades. ElastoDyn consequently attempts to open a nonexistent fourth blade-property filename; because no BldFile(4) exists, the diagnostic contains an invalid or unreadable filename. I corrected NumBl from 4 to 3 and retained the three supplied blade files. After that repair, the next run reaches InflowWind and reports the second independent fault: broken_inflow.dat sets WindType to 99, outside the documented valid range 1 through 7. This case uses the supplied TurbSim binary, so I corrected WindType to 3 and retained FileName_BTS as 5MW_Baseline/Wind/90m_12mps_twr.bts. The final fixed.fst explicitly references fixed_elasto.dat and fixed_inflow.dat. The complete fixed run reaches 60 seconds and terminates normally, proving that neither broken module was bypassed and that both identified input errors were repaired.
