555 astable timer assembly brief

Create a small astable timer schematic in bare.sch and save it as answer.sch.

Parts and values:
  U1: 555 timer
  R1: 10k timing resistor
  R2: 47k timing resistor
  C1: 100n timing capacitor
  C2: 10u supply capacitor
  LED1: red output indicator

Use these net names and native EAGLE 7.7 555 pin assignments:
  VCC: U1 V+ and reset R, plus the supply side of R1 and C2
  GND: U1 GND, C1, C2, and LED1 cathode C/K as provided by the selected LED symbol
  TRIG_THRES: U1 TR and THR, C1, and R2
  DISCH: U1 DIS, R1, and R2
  OUT: U1 Q and LED1 anode A
  CTRL: U1 CV

Place U1 centrally, the timing network to the left, and LED1 on the output side.
