QFN-48 Thermal Pad Repair
=========================

Open `qfn48_nominal_seed.kicad_mod` in KiCad Footprint Editor and repair the
manufacturing details of the center exposed-pad region.

The 48 peripheral pads and their numbering are already correct. Use
`qfn48_spec.csv` as the nominal target and save the finished footprint as:

    output/qfn48_nominal.kicad_mod
