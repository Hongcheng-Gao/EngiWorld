1. The product grad(u)*grad(v) is not the scalar inner product required by the Poisson bilinear form. Replace it with inner(grad(u), grad(v))*dx.
2. The original script writes only result.pvd and never creates the required fixed_summary.txt. After solving, evaluate the requested quantities and write both comma-separated values to that file.
3. A DOLFIN Function is evaluated at a Point object, not with u_sol(0.5, 0.5). Use u_sol(Point(0.5, 0.5)); obtain the global nodal maximum from u_sol.vector().get_local().
